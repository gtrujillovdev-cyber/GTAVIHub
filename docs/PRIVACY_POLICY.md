# Privacy Policy — GTAVIHubExtra

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

**GTAVIHubExtra** ("the Application") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with applicable privacy laws and platform API policies.

1. **Data Collected:** The Application only stores OAuth 2.0 authorization tokens (`access_token`, `refresh_token`, `open_id`) belonging to the authorized channel owner in order to upload and manage video posts via official APIs (TikTok Content Posting API, YouTube Data API v3).
2. **Data Usage:** Tokens and basic account metadata are used strictly to publish scheduled video content to the owner's authorized channels.
3. **No Data Sharing or Sale:** No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, advertisers, or external analytics brokers.
4. **Data Retention & Deletion:** OAuth tokens are stored locally in encrypted/private environment storage and can be revoked or deleted at any time from the platform's account settings or by contacting `gabtru@hotmail.com`.
